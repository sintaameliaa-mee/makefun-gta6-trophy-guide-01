Platinum roadmap — about 200 hours, one playthrough

All 55 trophies unlock in single player. There are no online trophies, no difficulty trophies and no mission medals. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission (15 manual slots plus one autosave). Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off your trophy save. Sources: PowerPyx, GTABase, Output Lag.

⚠ The one missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude

Every launch guide flags the same single risk. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning — she says she is going to meditate, heads to the balcony of Jason's house, and you follow her and choose Accept. That balcony meditation disappears after Chapter 1, and it is unconfirmed whether the trophy strictly needs it — so do it anyway, and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. Sources: PowerPyx, GTABase, KeenGamer, Output Lag.

  1. Stage 1 — Finish the story 60–80 hours

    Play the story through Chapters 1–4 to the final mission. Along the way: do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation; start stranger and character mission chains as soon as they unlock (some may have windows tied to story progress); scan unfamiliar vehicles and animals with WAiNK as you travel — progress made during the story already counts toward the 100% checklist, even though the checklist itself only appears after the credits.

  2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the 35-item checklist. It is lenient — roughly 60% true completion. Required in full: all robberies (39 Gang Operations, 22 Bank Jobs, 7 Payday Loan Jobs, 8 bank vaults), 33 underwater discoveries, all 42 Shooting Range gold medals, all drone races and Bret's quests. The rest is partial: 70 of 80 challenges, 300 of 397 stunts, 15 of 18 card sets, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 15 of 18 meditations. Most trophies pop along the way. Sources: PowerPyx, GTABase, EGamers, CryptoGames.

  3. Stage 3 — Cleanup and the platinum 10–20 hours

    Finish the remaining stranger strands (8 for Strangers & Freaks), the leftover miscellaneous trophies, and any guides marked unconfirmed once the community verifies them. When the last one pops, Vice Got a Price! — the platinum — is yours.
