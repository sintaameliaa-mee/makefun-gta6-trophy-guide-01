    ⚠ The one trophy you can miss

    Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze) — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The first session is Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth. Do it before Chapter 1 ends: it is the only trophy-related moment reported as possibly missable. Whether the trophy strictly needs that session is unconfirmed, so do it anyway and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. Sources: HowToVideoGame, PowerPyx, Output Lag.

      How this guide works

      • Official list: names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them, cross-checked by Exophase and PSNProfiles (PlanetGTA6, 18 Nov 2026). 28 of the 55 are hidden on PlayStation until earned; this guide flags them.
      • Own words, cited: every guide is written for this page and names its sources beside it. Five trophies have no confirmed trigger yet at launch — the guide says so instead of guessing.
      • No online trophies: all 55 are single-player. There are no difficulty trophies and no mission medals.
      • No mission replay: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one from just before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving.
      • Offline: this page loads nothing from the network. Export saves your checklist as a file; import brings it back.
